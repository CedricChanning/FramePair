# FramePair 0.3

FramePair 0.3 focuses on flexible photo arrangements and export preparation:

- Mix multiple arrangements in one batch: Direct keeps one photo as one output; Merge 2 and Merge 3 stack two or three photos vertically; Split divides one wide photo at its visual center into left and right outputs
- View candidate photos and merge results together in Merge Photos, replace an individual photo, fill an empty position, and exchange the top and bottom photos
- Track pixel dimensions and aspect ratios across source photos, arrangements, and Output Preview
- Optionally use Auto Crop to remove a few edge pixels when a photo is only slightly outside a common ratio
- Reorder finished arrangements in Output Preview and export them with automatic sequential numbering
- Apply a per-file JPEG size limit and review the estimated size and encoding quality before export
- Mix JPEG, PNG, and single-image TIFF sources in one batch, then export the batch as JPEG or lossless PNG
- Use the interface in English or Simplified Chinese and check for updates from within the application
